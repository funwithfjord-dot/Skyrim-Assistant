Healing & Recovery

Basic recipes for health, stamina, and Magicka recovery, regeneration, disease and poison treatment, and carry capacity. Open an ingredient pool to see gatherable alternatives, compatibility warnings, and specialty recipes.

Effects Ingredients Use Case Nice to Haves
Restore Health Blue Mountain Flower + Butterfly Wing Essential Basic immediate healing using gatherable ingredients. Pick Blue Mountain Flowers and catch orange butterflies. Open the ingredient pool for substitutes and useful pairings.
Healing ingredient pool: Combine two different ingredients below. Green indicates compatibility within this listed pool; yellow identifies a pairing to avoid. Purple rows are complete specialty recipes. Include a third ingredient only when it improves magnitude, duration, or useful effects.
Restore Health Blue Mountain Flower Gather flowers while traveling or grow your own. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Wheat also adds Fortify Health.
Restore Health Butterfly Wing Catch orange butterflies, not blue butterflies. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Blue Mountain Flower or Wheat gives healing only.
Restore Health Wheat Harvest from farms or grow your own. Avoid Swamp Fungal Pod: that pairing adds Lingering Damage Magicka. Butterfly Wing gives healing only; Blue Mountain Flower also adds Fortify Health.
Restore Health Blue Dartwing Collect blue dragonflies near water. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Swamp Fungal Pod or Ash Hopper Jelly also adds Resist Shock.
Restore Health Charred Skeever Hide Collect from cooked skeevers, not ordinary dead skeevers. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Ash Hopper Jelly also adds Restore Stamina; Felsaad Tern Feathers also adds Cure Disease.
Restore Health Rock Warbler Egg Harvest nests in rocky regions, especially the Reach. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Keep as an opportunistic substitute.
Restore Health Swamp Fungal Pod Gather in Hjaalmarch's marshes. Avoid Wheat: that pairing adds Lingering Damage Magicka. Blue Dartwing or Ash Hopper Jelly also adds Resist Shock.
Restore Health Ash Hopper Jelly Dragonborn / Solstheim Loot ash hoppers. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Blue Dartwing or Swamp Fungal Pod also adds Resist Shock; Charred Skeever Hide also adds Restore Stamina.
Restore Health Felsaad Tern Feathers Dragonborn / Solstheim Collect from Felsaad Terns. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Charred Skeever Hide also adds Cure Disease.
Restore Health + longer-duration Fortify Health Blue Mountain Flower + Wheat + Giant's Toe Specialty recipe using a Giant's Toe already collected. Normally extends Fortify Health from 60 to 300 seconds. It does not increase immediate healing. Giant's Toe is not an interchangeable healing ingredient.
Regenerate Health Garlic + Juniper Berries Important for Survival Supports health recovery over time rather than immediate healing. Collect Garlic from kitchens and Juniper Berries while exploring. Keep Restore Health potions available for emergencies.
Health regeneration ingredient pool: Combine two different ingredients below. Green indicates compatibility within this listed pool; yellow identifies a pairing to avoid. Purple rows are complete specialty recipes. Include a third ingredient only when it improves magnitude, duration, or useful effects.
Regenerate Health Garlic Collect hanging Garlic braids and loose kitchen supplies. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Keep a separate reserve for cooking.
Regenerate Health Juniper Berries Gather from juniper trees, especially in the Reach. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Angelfish also adds Fortify Archery.
Regenerate Health Luna Moth Wing Catch luna moths while exploring at night. Avoid Namira's Rot and Nordic Barnacle: either pairing adds Damage Magicka. Vampire Dust also adds Invisibility.
Regenerate Health Namira's Rot Gather fungus from cave floors. Avoid Luna Moth Wing and Nordic Barnacle: either pairing adds Damage Magicka. Garlic or Juniper Berries is a clean pairing.
Regenerate Health Nordic Barnacle Gather barnacle clusters around submerged ruins and shipwrecks. Avoid Luna Moth Wing and Namira's Rot: either pairing adds Damage Magicka. Angelfish also adds Waterbreathing.
Regenerate Health Vampire Dust Save drops from defeated vampires. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Luna Moth Wing also adds Invisibility. Treat as encounter loot rather than a bulk gathering staple.
Regenerate Health Emperor Parasol Moss Dragonborn / Solstheim Gather while exploring Solstheim. Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Keep as a regional substitute.
Regenerate Health Angelfish Official Creation / Fishing Compatible with every other ingredient in this pool. Nordic Barnacle also adds Waterbreathing; Juniper Berries also adds Fortify Archery. Save for useful specialty combinations.
Regenerate Health + Invisibility Luna Moth Wing + Vampire Dust A two-ingredient stealth-and-recovery potion. Useful for retreating or repositioning while supporting recovery. A third ingredient is unnecessary unless it adds another useful effect.
Regenerate Health + Waterbreathing Angelfish + Nordic Barnacle Official Creation / Fishing Combines recovery support with underwater breathing. Use when exploring submerged locations with an Angelfish available.
Regenerate Health + Fortify Archery Angelfish + Juniper Berries Official Creation / Fishing Situational archer preparation. Combines regeneration support with an archery buff using only two ingredients.
Restore Stamina Bee + Purple Mountain Flower Immediate stamina recovery for sprinting, power attacks, and escaping dangerous encounters. Collect Bees and Purple Mountain Flowers while exploring. —
Regenerate Stamina Mora Tapinella + Scaly Pholiota Supports stamina recovery during extended activity. Useful for repeated attacks or frequent sprinting. —
Restore Magicka Red Mountain Flower + Mora Tapinella Immediate Magicka recovery for spellcasting. Gather Red Mountain Flowers and Mora Tapinella while traveling. —
Regenerate Magicka Garlic + Salt Pile Supports Magicka regeneration during longer encounters. Keep a separate supply of both ingredients for cooking. —
Cure Disease Hawk Feathers + Vampire Dust Disease treatment without unrelated buffs. Save gathered feathers and vampire drops for a small reserve. —
Cure Poison Imp Gall + Scrib Jelly AE / Rare Curios Stops ongoing poison effects rather than providing resistance. These ingredients are typically purchased from Khajiit caravans; this entry is an exception to the gather-first preference. —
Fortify Carry Weight Creep Cluster + Hawk Beak Survival Temporary carrying capacity for loot and heavy materials. Plan where to unload before the effect expires. —

Cold-Region Rule

Prioritize hot soup, warm equipment, and planned shelter stops over optional loot or duplicate equipment.

Before Leaving Town

  • Sleep before a long trip.
  • Eat and replenish cooked food.
  • Pack hot soup for cold routes.
  • Restock healing and disease treatment.
