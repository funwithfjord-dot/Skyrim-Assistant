Skyrim Alchemy

A practical reference for potions, poisons, food, warmth, ingredients, crafting supplies, and optional recipe upgrades in Skyrim Anniversary Edition Survival Mode.

Core Expedition Kit

This is the standard loadout for a normal Survival Mode expedition. Increase the amount of food, hot soup, healing, and disease cures for long trips, northern travel, Solstheim, Dwemer ruins, or major dungeons.

Healing & Recovery

Basic recipes for restoring health, stamina, and Magicka; supporting regeneration; curing disease and poison; and temporarily increasing carry capacity. Optional ingredient upgrades will be reviewed individually.

Effects Ingredients Use Case Nice to Haves
Restore Health Blue Mountain Flower + Butterfly Wing Essential Basic immediate healing with easy-to-collect ingredients. Gather Blue Mountain Flowers while traveling and catch orange butterflies for Butterfly Wings. —
Regenerate Health Garlic + Juniper Berries Improves health regeneration over time rather than restoring health immediately. Keep separate from emergency healing potions. Reserve some Garlic for cooking. —
Restore Stamina Bee + Purple Mountain Flower Immediate stamina recovery for sprinting, power attacks, and escaping dangerous encounters. Gather Purple Mountain Flowers and collect Bees while exploring. —
Regenerate Stamina Mora Tapinella + Scaly Pholiota Supports stamina recovery during extended activity. Useful when you expect repeated attacks or frequent sprinting rather than needing a single immediate refill. —
Restore Magicka Red Mountain Flower + Mora Tapinella Immediate Magicka recovery for spellcasting. Collect Red Mountain Flowers along roads and Mora Tapinella from fallen logs and tree stumps. —
Regenerate Magicka Garlic + Salt Pile Supports Magicka regeneration during longer encounters. Both ingredients also serve as cooking supplies, so keep a separate reserve for Survival Mode meals. —
Cure Disease Hawk Feathers + Vampire Dust Treats diseases without adding unrelated buffs. Keep a small reserve for infections acquired while exploring. These ingredients are less convenient to gather than flowers. —
Cure Poison Imp Gall + Scrib Jelly AE / Rare Curios Stops ongoing poison effects. This is a cure, not preventive poison resistance. Purchase the ingredients from Khajiit caravans when available. —
Fortify Carry Weight Creep Cluster + Hawk Beak Survival Temporarily increases carrying capacity for loot extraction, heavy crafting materials, or the return trip from a dungeon. The effect is temporary, so plan where to unload. —

Survival Food & Warmth

Potions do not solve hunger, fatigue, or cold. Eat cooked food for hunger, sleep in a bed for fatigue, and use hot soup, fires, shelter, torches, and warm armor to survive cold regions.

Goal Food / Tool Reason to Carry or Use Nice to Haves
Cold recovery Hot Soup made with Fire Salts Essential Restores warmth and reduces cold. —
Emergency warmth Torch Provides warmth while traveling, but does not replace shelter or hot soup. —
Most hunger per weight Salmon Steak, Seared Slaughterfish, Rabbit Haunch, Steamed Mudcrab Legs Light travel rations that restore substantial hunger while weighing very little. —
Large meal / combat food Crab Stew High hunger restoration and long-duration health/stamina regeneration benefits. —
Stamina meal Vegetable Soup Useful sustained Stamina regeneration for travel and repeated power attacks. —
Health + stamina meal Beef Stew Useful before extended combat or a difficult dungeon section. —
Disease utility meal Garlic Bread Cures disease while also restoring hunger. —
Fatigue recovery Sleep in a bed Survival The real answer to fatigue. —
Food safety Cooked food Avoid raw meat Raw meat and seafood can cause Food Poisoning. —

Cold-Region Rule

Before traveling north, bring hot soup before carrying extra vendor loot or optional buff potions. A torch buys time, but a fire, forge, cave, inn, home, or indoor location is the reliable reset.

Before Leaving Town

  • Sleep first to remove fatigue penalties.
  • Eat until Well Fed.
  • Cook food and hot soup for the intended route.
  • Carry disease cures before heading to swamps, caves, or creature dens.

Poisons & Enemy Control

In Survival Mode, poisons are often more valuable than raw damage buffs because they can stop an enemy long enough for you to heal, retreat, reload, reposition, or escape.

Purpose Ingredients Effects Practical Use Nice to Haves
Best universal control poison Canis Root + Imp Stool + Mora Tapinella Paralysis + Lingering Damage Health Essential Use on bosses, dangerous melee targets, or surprise threats. —
Cheaper paralysis Canis Root + Imp Stool Paralysis Great standard control poison when you want to save Mora Tapinella. —
Slow Deathbell + Salt Pile Slow Sabre cats, charging melee enemies, and fast targets. —
Basic damage health Imp Stool + Skeever Tail Damage Health Cheap early-game poison for routine weapon use. —
Premium damage poison Chokeberry + Deathbell Damage Health + Weakness to Poison AE / Rare Curios Strong against high-health targets. —
Lingering damage poison Chokeberry + Mora Tapinella Lingering Damage Health AE / Rare Curios Apply, disengage, and let the effect work. —
Enemy infighting Hagraven Feathers + Troll Fat Frenzy Strong against groups that can turn on one another. —
Magic-damage setup Dreugh Wax + Weakness to Magic partner Weakness to Magic AE / Rare Curios Apply before a destruction magic burst. —

Recommended Weapon Poison Stack

  • 5–10 Canis Root + Imp Stool + Mora Tapinella paralysis poisons.
  • 5 Deathbell + Salt Pile slow poisons.
  • Optional premium stack: Chokeberry + Deathbell for tough enemies.
