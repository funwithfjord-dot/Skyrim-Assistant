Cold-Region Rule

Prioritize hot soup, warm equipment, and planned shelter stops over optional loot or duplicate equipment.

Before Leaving Town

  • Sleep before a long trip.
  • Eat and replenish cooked food.
  • Pack hot soup for cold routes.
  • Restock healing and disease treatment.
